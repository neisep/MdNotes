Where MdNotes should look first

Most of this backlog depends on subsystems MdNotes does not have — Bases, Canvas, Publish, Sync, mobile, plugins. These are the ones with real Obsidian demand that MdNotes could ship against its current architecture. Items already marked Implemented drop off the list, so what is left is what to build next.

    How to read the numbers. Obsidian has no formal vote counter; the forum uses likes on a topic as the vote, which is why counts here are likes on the whole thread rather than a single tally. Views and reply counts are included because a request with 120k views and 185 likes (Mermaid sizing) says something different from one with 3k views and the same likes.

    Currency. Pulled from forum.obsidian.md/c/feature-requests/8 on 17 September 2026, cross-checked against the official Obsidian roadmap. Three of these are now on that roadmap as planned or in progress and are marked as such.

    MdNotes status is my read against the app as it stands in PLAN.md: folder tree, Edit/Split/Reading, quick switcher, wikilinks, native Mermaid, full-text search, fixed 750 ms autosave — no tabs, backlinks, tags, properties, outline, folding, templates or export. That badge records the assessment made on the research date and is not revised as work lands; rows MdNotes has shipped since are the ones marked Implemented.
